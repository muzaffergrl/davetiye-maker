## The opening (index → home)

The catalogue cover layout: "Together with their families" and the names above, the envelope in the middle, the date ·
the city and "Tap to open" below, Skip in the corner. The envelope is a **portrait kraft envelope with a straight
flap**, held shut by a **belly band of six cut-paper flags** — no V flap, no wax seal (third wave: the envelope differs
from 01–32 in type, closure and choreography). On tap (`site.js MOTION.envelope`, same numbers as the `.is-opening`
keyframes in `site.css`):

| ms | step |
|---|---|
| 0–400 | "Tap to open" goes, the date line dims (the names stay) |
| 250–750 | the band works loose: its six flags shift apart and tilt (`band-loose`, `--spread`, `--tilt`) |
| 700–2250 | flag *i* lifts off at 700 + *i*·110 ms and flies 700 ms up to its place on a garland across the top of the opening (`flag-fly`; `layoutFlight()` writes `--tx`/`--ty`/`--r`/`--s` from the envelope to a sagging cord), then waves on its own phase (`wave`, a CSS wave that also leans with `--wind`) |
| 1500–2150 | the straight flap swings up on its top edge (3D `rotateX` 0 → 180°) and shows its lining (GPT: embroidered fabric; interim: terracotta) |
| 1900–3000 | the string lights light bulb by bulb under the garland (GPT bulbs only — none interim) and a warm light rises behind the envelope (light only) |
| 2500–3100 | the card rises out of the pocket |
| 3100–3480 | it comes forward |
| 3600 | home (view transition `invite-card` → the home hero card) |

Pocket, flap outside, flap lining, each flag, each bulb and the card are separate layers. Reduced motion: no opening,
straight to home. Without JS: the "Open the envelope" link. Opened state (session key `invite:opened:<slug>`): flags in
the garland, flap open, card forward, "Enter" + "Replay the opening".

## Inner pages

- **Photos hang in cut-paper frames** (`.hang.frame`): as the scene enters the view the frame drops in and swings to
  rest like hanging paper (`swing-in`, a damped pendulum), then sways in the breeze (`breeze`); scrolling raises the
  wind (`initWind`: scroll speed → `--wind` on `<html>`, decays each frame) and frames and flags lean with it. Distinct
  from 30's tiles and 13's polaroids. Interim frame = a mat of tissue paper (a plain crop) round the photo; the GPT
  frame (W10/W11) replaces it with the photo in its measured `#00FF00` window.
- **Text unfurls** from its top edge like a flag (`rotateX(-78deg)` → none, with a small flutter).
- **home:** the garland of six flags (wave) + lights, the cream card (portrait in frame 1, names, date, venue;
  bougainvillea corners when W12 exists), the invitation on a cream band edged with embroidered fabric (W13), countdown,
  calendar, story teaser (frame 2), the day on a terracotta band, the signature (lights, names, replay).
- **story:** a small garland, three moments left–right–left in frames 1–3.
- **details:** the order of the day on kraft paper; each row starts with a small flag that swings in (`.row-flag`,
  colours cycling 1–6); the venue photo in the wide frame; G127 map; G138 blocks, travel, menu; registry; FAQ.
- **rsvp:** the G042 form with G051 questions and G069 guest names on a cream card under a small garland; on success a
  handful of the flags flutter down over the thank-you (`particles('reply')`).
- Thank-you mode (G075), G144 language labels (new site strings in `lib/i18n/dict/site/*`), `scroll-padding-bottom`
  for the bottom nav.

## Built from the GPT parts (2026-10-22)

All PAKET 5 parts are used (`img/manifest.json` `parts.used` = 17, `waiting_for_gpt` empty); "Interim pictures" above now
describes only the look of a missing part. The demo shows the design's PAKET 2 photos (`public/demo/papel-picado-fiesta/*`,
`lib/claim/demo-photos.json`, `node scripts/build-demo-photos.ts --theme papel-picado-fiesta`). Eye check fixes:

- **Envelope.** `envelope_pocket` came 1079×1458 (alpha box 0.75:1, the asked stage 0.74) and `envelope_flap` 3.30:1
  instead of the asked 100 × 30 box (2.47:1), so the flap was stretched 1.34× in height. The stage is now the pocket's
  own shape (0.750:1, `manifest stage`; the asked boxes in `stage_asked`), the flap spans the width at its own height
  (22.75 %), the liner at its own (102 % of the flap); `--env-ratio` / `--flap-h` drive the stage size and the room kept
  above it for the opened flap. The flag band keeps its box (over the flap's lower edge).
- **Frames.** `frame_3`'s green window had a transparent middle inside a soft green rim; the first build keyed only the
  green and a green line stayed round the photo. Now the whole measured rectangle is cleared and the spill neutralised
  (as silver-photo-booth); no key green in any frame. The green left in `flag_4` / `tissue_4` (a green flag), the
  embroidery, the liner and the bloom is the parts' own colour. Frames at q 80 (`frame_3` was 266 kB).
- **Bougainvillea.** GPT left a faint alpha haze round the cluster (≈ 26 000 pixels at alpha 1–7, out to 6 % beyond it):
  alpha below 12 is cleared before the trim (`dehaze`).
- **Card.** The texts are sized by the card (cqw); `fitCard` keeps the last line above the card's bottom margin
  (40-character names fit at 360×640).

## Known limits

- Checked on 2026-10-22 in Chromium only (demo route at 360×640 and 390×844: closed, the opening seeked frame by frame,
  all pages); not `theme-check.cjs` on a production build.
- Not tried on a real iPhone/Safari or Android Chrome; `rotate` + `transform` together and `var()` inside keyframes
  need a current browser (older ones skip the lean, the swing still plays).
- The garland's cord is part of each GPT flag (its folded top edge over a twine); interim rectangles hang without one.
