# Crimson Rose Atelier (21)

Independent wedding website theme: crimson roses, a burgundy envelope with a gold botanical wax seal, deckled ivory
cards with gold borders, candlelit oxblood. Codex G133 → theme standard G142 → **atelier redesign 2026-10-23** (user:
"from 21 on, everything must look like a professional designer made it — own design, components, animation and
sound"). Status: **live** (`lib/marketing/config.ts`).

## v2 — 2026-10-24: the GPT design board, approved by the user

The 2026-10-23 build was still flatter than GPT's own board (`collections/21_Crimson_Rose_Atelier/assets/gpt/mockups/
design-board.png`). v2 follows that board screen by screen and wires the PAKET 8 GPT parts:

| | v2 |
|---|---|
| Type | **Bodoni Moda** (Didone) for the names, big headings and figures; every label and button in spaced Cormorant capitals; Corinthia only for the ampersand and handwritten lines; Manrope dropped. Names fallback faces re-measured (`scripts/font-fallback.ts`) |
| Letter | the envelope lies **tilted and large at the foot of the screen**, running off its edge, a gold sprig at the seal, on velvet with satin below; the tap straightens it and lifts it to the middle (CSS transition) before the seal cracks; petals fall from the seal |
| Home | the board's first screen: hosts' line, the names in tall Didone capitals with a gold script ampersand and the request on candlelit velvet; the **torn oxblood paper** gives way to ivory and the **oval portrait** with a single rose sits across the tear. The photograph in the envelope's card flies into it (view transition `invite-card`, named on the photograph) |
| Story | the leaves are GPT **album pages** (binding on the left) with the silk **ribbon** bookmark |
| Details / RSVP | Didone titles with a gold rule; rose corners at the foot of the day; the RSVP roses moved from over the title to the card's foot |
| Page boxes | full width and clipping (the column inside keeps its width): roses run to the screen's edge and are cut by it, never by a column; nothing widens the page |

PAKET 9 (prompts in `GPT_PROMPTS.md`, hooks ready in `build_images.py`): gold oval frame (window may be transparent),
programme icons, album photo corners, gold flourish, satin bow, rosebud.

## What changed in the redesign

| | Before (G142) | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | seal lifts, flat flap, plain ivory rectangle rises | seal **cracks in two** (clip-path halves fall left/right), flap turns in **3D with both faces** (front shaded, inside showing), the GPT **deckled card** is drawn out rose first, then **flies to the guest** while the envelope sinks out of focus (Web Animations), candle glow, gold glint on the seal, the envelope leans towards the pointer |
| Home | names on a burgundy block + oval photo | the card from the envelope lands as the hero (view transition), lies back as you scroll (scroll-driven animation), handwritten epigraph that writes itself, formal invitation on the second GPT card (3-slice), odometer countdown, fanned album prints, venue photo opening like an iris |
| Story | stacked leaves + arrows | a little **book**: three deckled leaves turned in 3D by finger (drag), buttons, arrows or thumbnails; light slides across the turning page |
| Details | typographic list | the Order of the Day hangs on a **gold thread that draws itself** as you scroll; each moment lights up when the thread reaches it; the venue photo rises out of the paper |
| RSVP | cream card | tall deckled reply card (3-slice); on send a **little envelope receives a card, closes and is sealed** |
| Sound | shared effects + "Evening Strings" | **own sound pack** (`public/audio/fx/crimson-rose-atelier/`) and **own music** "Rose Nocturne" (`tools/audio_packs.py`) |

## Images (`build_images.py`)

- `env_closed` the closed envelope cropped to its body = the stage (ratio 1.8707); pocket and both flap faces are
  clip-path cuts of it (flap tip at 76.5 %).
- `env_inside` the open envelope's body below its hinge, scaled onto the closed body; its pocket V (64 %) is lifted
  onto the closed one in CSS (`height:119.5%`).
- `card_portrait` = `parts/info_card.png` (rose crown, free 13–87 % × 20–88 %): the env card, the home hero card, the
  album leaves, the RSVP card. `card_formal` = `parts/invitation_card.png` (roses top-left and bottom-right): the formal
  invitation. Both have a `_band` crop (a plain stretch, no flourish) that repeats when a card must grow (3-slice).
- `paper` a seamless 2 × 2 mirror tile of the paper texture (no seams when repeated). `rose`, `seal`, `ph_*`.
- PAKET 8 (`GPT_PROMPTS.md` W3.1–W3.11, ordered 2026-10-23): velvet, satin, petals ×5, gold oval frame (green window →
  `mask_oval.png`), torn edge, ribbon, gold sprig, album page, rose corner, rose bloom. When they land:
  `python3 tools/gpt_yerlestir.py …` → `python3 platform/themes/crimson-rose-atelier/build_images.py` →
  add their part keys to `collections/21_Crimson_Rose_Atelier/variants/recolor.json` → `recolor_parts.py build --design 21`.

## Sound

`DESIGN_SOUND['crimson-rose-atelier']`: `pack` = its own folder; opening cues above; page cues via
`document.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('invite:sfx', {detail: {name}}))`: home `petal` (arrival, only with petals),
story `page` (every turn), rsvp `fold` + `stamp` (sealing); the platform plays `tap` (links) and `chime` (reply sent)
from the same pack. Music: `rose-nocturne` (story page; design-only track, `MUSIC_TRACKS[].theme`).

## Deviations / limits

- No PAKET 8 parts yet: no velvet (CSS candlelight on the paper grain instead), no petals, no gold oval frame (the oval
  photo has a hairline inset shadow), straight sheet edge instead of torn paper, no bookmark ribbon.
- The env card's miniature type is pure `cqw` (under 12 px while it is small, aria-hidden); the home card has a 12 px
  floor on its date line.
- Scroll-driven "the card lies back" runs only where `animation-timeline: view()` exists (Chrome/Edge, Safari 26).
- Not tried: real iPhone / Android, Vercel deployment, real buyer photos, slow network, real speakers/headphones for
  the sounds (rendered and checked as spectrograms only).
