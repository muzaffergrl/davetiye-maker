## 2. Description

---
IVORY PEARL ATELIER
An understated wedding invitation in ivory cotton paper, champagne silk and fine gold edges, finished with a luminous mother-of-pearl seal.

TRY THE LIVE DEMO
https://bkestudio.com/start/demo/ivory-pearl-atelier
Sample names and photographs; the couple and venue are fictional.

A DIFFERENT FIRST MOMENT
Your guests tap the envelope. The pearl turns and moves in an arc to the side while the silk bow drifts apart. The flap lifts, and the invitation rises with a gentle tilt into the home page. Guests can skip the opening; reduced-motion settings are respected.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
An animated envelope opening and four pages: Home, Our Story, Details and RSVP. Your own online editor, five photo spots, a countdown, calendar links, directions, travel and accommodation information, a menu, questions and answers, online RSVP, a private guest list and Excel or CSV downloads. Share your website by link or a QR code. Download a website PDF or a printable invitation from your editor. A START HERE PDF guides you through the setup.

MAKE IT YOURS
Change your names, wedding date, venue, photographs, story, programme, questions, headings and labels. Choose music, upload your own music or turn it off. The artwork, colours, fonts and animation are part of this design and stay as shown. No Canva account or software installation is required.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Purchase the digital product and download START_HERE.pdf from Etsy.
2. Open the start link and enter your Etsy order number and email. Order confirmation may be required before publishing.
3. Personalize, publish and share your website. Continue on another device with the same order number and email.

GOOD TO KNOW
This is a digital product; nothing is shipped. Your website remains online until 12 months after your wedding. You can request an extension through the shop before that date. Names and date have the platform editing limits described in START HERE. English, Turkish, Spanish, German and French interfaces are available. Photographs in the demo are fictional examples; upload your own. Some visual artwork was created with AI tools and assembled into this original design.

QUESTIONS?
Message STUDIOBKE on Etsy. BKE Studio replies within 24 hours. Digital orders cannot be returned, exchanged or cancelled, but please contact us if something does not work as described.
