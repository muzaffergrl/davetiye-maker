---

One more round of changes to your custom wedding website design.

This listing is only for couples who already ordered a custom design from our shop. Your custom design includes 2 rounds of changes; buy this listing for each further round you would like. If you have not ordered a custom design, please message us before you buy.

WHAT YOU GET (ONE ROUND)
• One more round of changes to your custom design: the opening, artwork, colors or fonts
• Send us all the changes for this round together in one Etsy message
• We send you the updated design within 3 days of receiving your changes

WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED
• Your names, dates, photos and texts: you change those yourself in your editor at any time, free (or with our setup service)
• A completely new design idea: that is a new custom design order

GOOD TO KNOW
• One purchase = one round of changes. Buy it again for another round.
• Your design stays yours alone: we never sell it to anyone else.
• Digital service: nothing is printed or shipped. [VERIFY: Etsy's policy on service listings]
• No returns, exchanges or cancellations once we have started your changes. If a change we made does not match what you asked for, message us on Etsy and we will fix it.

QUESTIONS?
Message us here on Etsy. We reply within 24 hours.
