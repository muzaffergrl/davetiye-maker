## 2. Description

Paste from the line below.

---

**A wedding website that opens like a real envelope, with online RSVP built in. You fill it in yourself in an online editor and share one link with your guests.**

Blush peonies, garden roses and a soft pink envelope tied with a silk bow. Your guests tap the envelope, it opens, and your invitation follows: Home, Our Story, Details and RSVP.

**WHAT YOU GET**

- A wedding website in the Blush Garden Rose design: envelope opening, Home, Our Story, Details and RSVP pages.
- A web address with your names, for example …/clara-and-julian (you choose it when you start).
- An online editor made for phone screens that also works on a computer. [VERIFY: real iPhone Safari and Android Chrome] Change names, date, ceremony time, venue, the order of the day (up to 10 events), dress code, story captions, links and photos. Every change saves by itself; your guests see it after you press Publish.
- Your own photos: a portrait for the invitation card, three Our Story photos and a venue photo, each cropped to the design's shape in the editor.
- Online RSVP: guests reply on your website. You see every reply, the head count and the answers in your editor, and you can download all replies as a spreadsheet (CSV).
- Up to 3 questions for your guests in the RSVP form, for example a meal choice. [VERIFY: try on the live site]
- Add-to-calendar buttons (Apple / Outlook and Google Calendar).
- Questions & answers for your guests, up to 12, on the Details page. [VERIFY: try on the live site]
- A countdown to your day, map, travel & stay and registry buttons (with your own links).
- A QR code of your website to download (PNG or SVG) for printed cards and signs.
- A START HERE guide (PDF) with the start link and every step in pictures.

**HOW IT WORKS**

1. Buy and download the START HERE PDF from your Etsy purchases.
2. Open the start link in the PDF and enter your Etsy order number, email and first names. Your website starts as a private draft.
3. Save your edit link (we cannot email it to you yet), then fill in your details and add your photos.
4. Press Publish and share your link, or print the QR code.

No Canva account, no app and no design skills needed. Your guests need no app and no account either.

**PLEASE NOTE**

- This is a digital product. Nothing is printed or shipped.
- You change your names, date, times, venue, schedule, dress code, story captions, questions & answers, RSVP questions, links and photos. The design's headings (for example "You are invited", "Order of the Day", "Will You Join Us?"), the painted design and the colors stay as they are.
- The website's headings, buttons and RSVP form are in English. You can write your own texts (names, schedule, story captions, Q&A) in another language: letters with accents (é, ñ, ü) and Cyrillic use the design's fonts; other alphabets (for example Greek, Arabic or Chinese) may show in a plain standard font.
- One website per order. The web address cannot be changed after you start.
- Your website stays online until 12 months after your wedding date.
- A custom domain (for example yournames.com) is not included.
- No music is included.
- The sample names, dates, places and photos are fictional and are not part of your website.
- The painted design elements and the sample photos were made with AI image tools from our own descriptions, then edited and assembled by us. [VERIFY: Etsy AI disclosure wording and the "Designed by" setting]
- No returns, exchanges or cancellations: this is a digital product with instant access. If something on your website does not work as described, message us on Etsy and we will fix it.

**QUESTIONS?**
Message BKE Studio on Etsy or write to bkestudio@gmail.com. We answer within 24 hours.

## 7. FAQ (for the listing or the shop FAQ)

**Do I need Canva or an app?**
No. You fill in your website in your web browser, on your phone or computer. Your guests only need the link.

**How do I get my website after I buy?**
Download the START HERE PDF from your Etsy purchases. Open the start link inside it and enter your Etsy order number.

**Can I see it before I buy?**
Yes: open the live demo (image 8). It shows sample names and placeholder photos.

**What can I change?**
Your names, date, times, venue, schedule, dress code, story captions, questions & answers, RSVP questions, links and photos. The design's headings (for example "You are invited", "Order of the Day", "Will You Join Us?"), the painted design and the colors stay as they are.

**Is it available in other languages?**
The design's headings, buttons, RSVP form and calendar texts are in English. Your own texts can be in another language: letters with accents and Cyrillic use the design's fonts; other alphabets may show in a plain standard font. Dates can be written in US, UK, Canadian or Australian style.

**How do my guests reply?**
On your website's RSVP page. You see every reply in your editor and can download them as a spreadsheet (CSV).

**Can I change things after I publish?**
Yes. Edit, then press Publish changes.

**Can I use my own domain?**
Not at the moment. Your address is a link with your names.

**How long does my website stay online?**
Until 12 months after your wedding date.

**Is it printed or shipped?**
No. It is a digital product.

**I lost my edit link. What now?**
