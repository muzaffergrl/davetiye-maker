**Instructions for GPT (read fully before starting):**
- You are the **art director and illustrator** of this design. Aim for **high-end luxury wedding stationery**
  quality: rich detail, real paper and silk materials, believable soft shadows and depth, painterly florals,
  gold foil accents, generous breathing room. It must look like a premium, best-selling invitation — never
  flat clip-art, never generic vector shapes.
- Work in **one chat** for this whole package so every image shares the same envelope, flowers, paper, gold
  and light. Generate step T1 first; then use the T1 images as **reference images** for every T2 image.
- Technique for florals: **romantic oil painting** (soft visible brush texture). Paper, silk ribbon and gold
  are rendered realistically.
- Palette: ink `#3F2A2E` · deep rose `#9B5D66` · blush `#E3A9AE` · mist `#FBEDEC`→`#EFCFD0` · ivory `#FFF9F6`
  · gold `#C8A46E` · sage `#9DAE93`.
- Vertical images are 1024×1536 (2:3) but the video is 9:16: keep everything important inside the
  **central 85% of the width**; the outer ~7% on the left and right may be cropped.
- **T1 comps may contain text** (they are layout references only, never sold). **T2 images contain NO text,
  letters, numbers or monograms at all.**
- Where a photo will go, fill the window with **flat pure green #00FF00** (no texture, no shading) so Claude
  can place the buyer's photo exactly there.
- Must not resemble any real person, brand or another seller's design.
