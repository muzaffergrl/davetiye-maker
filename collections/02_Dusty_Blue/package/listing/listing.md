**A complete dusty blue wedding invitation set you edit yourself in Canva: a video invitation for phones, a one-page wedding website, a printable 5 × 7 invitation, and two ready-to-share images, all in one matching design.**

Dusty blue, ivory paper and champagne gold, with eucalyptus, white florals and a wax-seal envelope.
Classic, romantic and easy to personalise. No design experience needed.

**WHAT'S INCLUDED**

1. **Video invitation** — vertical 1080 × 1920 px, made for phone screens. The envelope opens, your
   photos appear one by one, then your invitation, schedule and RSVP details. [VERIFY: animation
   built and MP4 export checked — GA02] Download as MP4 and send by text, WhatsApp, email or social
   media.
2. **Wedding website** — one long page that works on phones and computers: welcome, Our Story,
   Details, Travel & Stay, Registry, RSVP and FAQ. Publish it yourself on a free Canva web address.
   [VERIFY: publish on free domain tested — GB02]
3. **RSVP form** — guests reply on your website using a Canva form; responses go to your own Canva
   account. [VERIFY: two-account test — responses reach only the buyer's copy — GB02]
4. **Printable invitation** — 5 × 7 in. Print at home or at a print shop, or send as a PDF with a
   clickable website link. [VERIFY: link clickable after PDF export]
5. **Story image** (1080 × 1920 px) and **square image** (1080 × 1080 px) for sharing in chats and
   stories.
6. **Step-by-step guide (PDF)** with your template links and clear instructions for editing, photos,
   video download, RSVP, publishing and printing.

**WHAT YOU CAN EDIT**

- All text: names, date, times, venue, schedule, dress code, RSVP date, story text, monogram.
- Your own photos (drag and drop into ready-made frames).
- Links on the website and in the PDF.
- Decorative pieces (envelope, wax seal, florals, paper textures) are image layers: you can move,
  resize or remove them, but not redraw or recolour them.

**HOW IT WORKS**

1. Purchase and download the PDF guide from your Etsy purchases page.
   [VERIFY: Etsy digital listing delivers the file after payment — Etsy-handled]
2. Click the template links in the guide. Each one opens your own copy in Canva.
3. Log in to Canva (a free account is enough [VERIFY: full set tested on Canva Free]) and edit.
4. Download the video, PDF and images; publish your website; share with your guests.

**PLEASE NOTE**

- This is a **digital product**. Nothing is shipped or printed for you.
- You edit the templates yourself in Canva. [PERSONALIZATION_SERVICE: offered or not — user to decide]
- You need a Canva account (free or paid) and an internet connection. A computer is recommended.
- **No music is included.** If you add music in Canva, check its licence before posting.
- **No fonts are included as files.** The fonts used are available inside Canva.
  [VERIFY: Pinyon Script, Cormorant Garamond, Montserrat free in Canva for Free accounts]
- A custom domain (for example yournames.com) is not included. Websites on a free Canva plan show a
  small "Designed with Canva" footer.
- The sample names, dates and places are fictional. Sample photos are placeholders and are not part
  of the download.
- Colours may look slightly different on screen and in print.
- Some decorative graphics and the sample photos were created with AI image tools from our own
  prompts, then selected, edited and arranged into this original design by us.
  [VERIFY: Etsy AI disclosure policy — wording and placement]
